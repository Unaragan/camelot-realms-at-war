# Albion Epic-Questline: Level 1–50 (design template)

> **Classification:** how [Frontier_NPCs_and_Questlines.md](Frontier_NPCs_and_Questlines.md) — forward planning for the later launch area/open world phase, **None** Replacement or anticipation of the current battleground vertical slice. **Place holders** (`[Albion-Region: Name TBD]`as long as the open world does not yet exist (see [OpenWorld_Frontier_3Realms_Vision.md](OpenWorld_Frontier_3Realms_Vision.md) Purpose: an end-to-end, story-driven main questline for Albion from Level 1 to 50, taking players through all future Albion regions and the battlegrounds already designed and culminating in epic rewards for RvR entry.

## Basic concept

26 quest levels over the full Level 1–50 arc (25 levels until the Final Quest becomes available, then the Final Quest itself), divided into 5 acts.

- **Levels 1–3 (AEQ-01 to AEQ-03) are the quests already generated by co-pilot** ("The Silent Frontier" / "Shadows Across the Border" / "To the Borderlands"), fertiger Content in [Quests.md](Quests.md) - here only rearranged as a prelude to the big sheet, not rewritten. [PROMPTS.md](../../PROMPTS.md).
- **Recurrent NPCs** Excl. [NPC_NAMES.md](../../NPC_NAMES.md) (Lord Ector, Burgvogt Kay) and [Frontier_NPCs_and_Questlines.md](Frontier_NPCs_and_Questlines.md) (Watch Captain Baldwin, head Eleanor) appear several times instead of just once – giving the journey narrative continuity.
- **The Three Staggered Battlegrounds** from the Frontier vision (~Lv 20 "Grenzland-Scharmützel", ~Lv 30 "Contested Highlands", ~Lv 50 "Bastion of the Kill-Zone") are installed directly as action stations, not only as a separate BG system on the side.
- **Three entities subject to group obligations** (Lv 16/28/39, see Act tables and [Quests.md](Quests.md) → “Instance Overview” with named boss and loot concept – formalize the existing group levels to real dungeon goals instead of just story beats.
- **New Antagonist:** "The Broken Oath" (**The Broken Oath**) - a heretical splinter group that emerged from (alleged) descendants of Albion's own order who once sealed the relic site. They believe the seal was a betrayal of the land and must be reversed - whatever the cost. **Shadow reflection of Albion's own custodian claim** (see Basic Conflict in NPC_NAMES.md) – they believe they are doing the right thing, only with opposite signs.
- **Leader:** **Sir Alaric**, the "Hollow Warden" - originally the knight who once personally sealed the relic site, since then unnaturally kept alive/immortal by his own seal magic, after centuries of solitary vigil falls mad. Wants to break the seal, even if it destroys himself - and perhaps the country. Name from the existing generic Albion name pool (see NPC_NAMES.md), not a reinvention.
- **Random find with added value:** The Wardstein weakness that worries Rí Aengus/Shreinkeeper Niav in Hibernia (see NPC_NAMES.md/Frontier_NPCs_and_Questlines.md) gets the same cause here – the Broken Oath saws at the seal across all three realms. No contradiction, true cross-realm continuity for later midgard/hibernia epics, without anticipating them here.

## Reward (concept, not a finished itemization system)

Epic, serves as a visible transition to the permanent RvR entry (not as a conclusion, but as "now the real war begins"):

- Unique, named anchored weapon/equipment related to the story (e.g. re-forged from Alaric’s broken seal artifact)
- Visible title (e.g. "Warden of the Seal") as status symbol in the RvR
- Consciously **None** finished number/stat design – this is part of the still open itemization question (see not-now list in README.md), here only anchored conceptually

### Formulated example: stage 5, *A Soldier's Instinct*

> "You are writing content for 'Camelot: Realms at War', a toon-styled 3-faction RvR fantasy game (spiritual successor to Dark Age of Camelot, but with no DAOC-specific names/lore reused). This is stage 5 of a 26-stage Albion main questline (level 1–50). Context so far: the player investigated missing border scouts and a border raid for Lord Ector and Burgvogt Kay, then joined a first border patrol. Write this quest: given by 'Guard-Captain Baldwin' (stoic, disciplined, quietly proud of his watch) at a border watchtower, for a level-8 character. Premise: the tower's signal fire was deliberately sabotaged — not an accident. Investigating the site, the player finds a strange rune-mark that matches neither of Albion's known rivals, hinting at a third, unknown party. Please generate, in English: quest title, a 2-3 sentence quest description, 3 objective steps, opening dialogue, closing dialogue. Tone: grounded, quietly unsettling rather than alarming — Baldwin doesn't yet grasp the scale of what he's found. No mention of Dark Age of Camelot or DAOC-specific proper nouns."
