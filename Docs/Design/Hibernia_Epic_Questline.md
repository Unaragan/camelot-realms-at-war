# Hibernia Epic-Questline: Level 1–50 (design template)

> **Classification:** how [Albion_Epic_Questline.md](Albion_Epic_Questline.md) — forward planning for the later launch area/open world phase, **None** Replacement or anticipation of the current battleground vertical slice. **Place holders** (`[Hibernia-Region: Name TBD]`Implementation status will be centralized in [Quests.md](Quests.md) tracked.

## Basic concept

Reflects the structure of the Albion and Midgard epic questlines (5 acts, 26 levels, level 1–50, 25 of them up to the final quest), with its own tone: Hibernia’s custodian claim (see basic conflict in NPC_NAMES.md) characterizes a **Natural Magic/Mystical** coined narrative instead of military or honor-related framing.

- **Recurrent NPCs:** Rí Aengus and Ceann Cogaidh Aoife (Military) [NPC_NAMES.md](../../NPC_NAMES.md), shrinekeeper Niav and head of Cormac [Frontier_NPCs_and_Questlines.md](Frontier_NPCs_and_Questlines.md).
- **The same three step-stage battlegrounds** (~Lv 20 “Borderland skirmishes”, ~Lv 30 “Contested Highlands”, ~Lv 50 “Bastion of the Kill Zone”) – as in Midgard deliberately told the same shared locations, from Hibernia’s perspective.
- **Three entities subject to group obligations** (Lv 16/28/39, see Act tables and [Quests.md](Quests.md) → "Instance Overview" with respectively named boss and loot concept.
- **New Antagonist:** **"The Withered Circle"** — a group once close to the Guardians who believe that every border/sealing (including that of the relic) is a wound on the land that can heal only by a complete return to the wilderness — if necessary, by wilderness/destruction of everything bound. Shadow reflection of Hibernia’s Guardianship claim: they also believe to protect the land only by dissolving all ties instead of preservation.
- **Leader:** **Fintan the Rootbound** -- once a Guardian Druid who tried to permanently weave himself with the land to better protect it. The ritual slipped away from him; he's been half human, half rampant roots ever since, and now believes that **all** Ties — including those around the relic site — are as damaging to the land as their own ritual has harmed them. Name from the generic Hibernia men’s pool (see NPC_NAMES.md), now reserved for this role.
- **Cross-realm continuity:** The same cause as the Siegel/Wardstein weakness in Albion and Midgard's epics. fits most directly in terms of content, as Rí Aengus and Niav's concern for Wardstein magic in NPC_NAMES.md/Frontier_NPCs_and_Questlines.md already anticipates this story.

### Formulated example: HEQ-01, *The Fading Grove*

> "You are writing content for 'Camelot: Realms at War', a toon-styled 3-faction RvR fantasy game (spiritual successor to Dark Age of Camelot, but with no DAOC-specific names/lore reused). This is stage 1 of a 26-stage Hibernia main questline (level 1-50). Write this quest: given by 'Rí Aengus' (Hibernia's ruler, sees itself as guardian rather than owner of the land, quietly worried rather than commanding) at Hibernia's home keep, for a level-1 character. Premise: a grove not far from the keep has lost its vitality — no visible cause, no signs of battle or disease, just a slow, wrong kind of fading. Aengus asks the player to go look, more out of unease than any concrete suspicion yet. Please generate, in English: quest title, a 2-3 sentence quest description, 3 objective steps, opening dialogue, closing dialogue. Tone: mystical, quietly unsettling, reverent towards nature — this is the entry point to a longer 20-stage questline, so end on an open, uneasy note rather than resolution. No mention of Dark Age of Camelot or DAOC-specific proper nouns."
