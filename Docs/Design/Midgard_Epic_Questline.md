# Midgard Epic-Questline: Level 1–50 (design template)

> **Classification:** how [Albion_Epic_Questline.md](Albion_Epic_Questline.md) — forward planning for the later launch area/open world phase, **None** Replacement or anticipation of the current battleground vertical slice. **Place holders** (`[Midgard-Region: Name TBD]`Implementation status will be centralized in [Quests.md](Quests.md) tracked.

## Basic concept

Reflects the structure of the Albion epic questline (5 acts, 26 levels, level 1-50, 25 of them up to the final quest), but consciously **different in tone** instead of a pure Reskin copy — Midgard’s claim to honor/conquest (see basic conflict in NPC_NAMES.md) shapes the narrative: less “investigation”, more **Test and open conflict**.

- **Recurrent NPCs:** Jarl Skadi (castle) and Hersir Vidar (military) [NPC_NAMES.md](../../NPC_NAMES.md) Guard Captain Gunnar and Mine Warden Ulf [Frontier_NPCs_and_Questlines.md](Frontier_NPCs_and_Questlines.md).
- **The same three step-stage battlegrounds** As with Albion (~Lv 20 "Borderland skirmishes", ~Lv 30 "Contested Highlands", ~Lv 50 "Bastion of the Kill Zone") — deliberately identical, since they are the same shared 3-rich battlegrounds, told only from Midgard's perspective. All three Empire epics run towards the same locations, for different motives – this is itself a narrative meeting point.
- **Three entities subject to group obligations** (Lv 16/28/39, see Act tables and [Quests.md](Quests.md) → "Instance Overview" with respectively named boss and loot concept.
- **New Antagonist:** **"The Ironbrood"** -- a splinter group that holds Midgard's own warrior code of honor for weakness. *Preservation* They discard something: the idea that honor counts in fighting. Shadow reflection of Midgard’s claim to conquest—they believe in the same goal (the relic belongs to the strongest), but without any limitation.
- **Commander:** **Ragnar Half-Sworn** — once Hersir Vidar's shield brother, broke his warrior to save his entourage from annihilation years ago, was disowned instead of honored. Since then convinced that honor is a lie that only protects the weak. Name from the generic Midgard men's pool (see NPC_NAMES.md), now reserved for this role. **Personal reference to Vidar** (former shield brothers) gives act 4 emotional weight without copying the structure of the Albion lineage.
- **Cross-realm continuity:** The same cause as the Wardstein/Seal weakness in Albions and Hibernia's Epics - The Ironbrood works, without knowing it, on the same larger tear in the seal as the other two factions; no combination of the three groups (this would remain a possible later meta-revelation, not fixed here), only the same wound in three places.

## Reward (concept)

As laid out epic with Albion, serves as a transition to the RvR entry: unique weapon/equipment related to the story (e.g., a newly forged splitter of Ragnar’s broken Eidring), visible title (e.g., “Oathkeeper” — conscious echo to the central theme).

[#] of a 26-stage Midgard main questline (level 1–50, stage 26 is the group finale). Write this quest for the Midgard faction: given by [NPCs] at [Local placeholders]for a level-[Lv] character. Premise: [Short content]. Tone: direct, honor-and-trial-driven, less ceremony than Albion — Midgard characters prove things through action, not long speeches. [This quest requires a group of 3–4 players, mention the boss by name if one is given.] Please generate, in English: quest title, a 2-3 sentence quest description, 3-5 objective steps, opening dialogue, closing dialogue. No mention of Dark Age of Camelot or DAOC-specific proper nouns."

### Formulated example: MEQ-05, *Gunnar's Watch*

> "You are writing content for 'Camelot: Realms at War', a toon-styled 3-faction RvR fantasy game (spiritual successor to Dark Age of Camelot, but with no DAOC-specific names/lore reused). This is stage 5 of a 26-stage Midgard main questline (level 1-50). Context so far: the player proved himself in Jarl Skadi's trials and joined Hersir Vidar's raiding party, then fought in a first border skirmish. Write this quest: given by 'Watch-Captain Gunnar' (blunt, dutiful, uneasy — not a man who spooks easily) at a border watchtower, for a level-8 character. Premise: Gunnar's watch post has gone completely silent — no fight, no fire, no tracks leading away, just an empty post. Investigating, the player finds no sign of a struggle at all, which unsettles Gunnar more than an obvious attack would. Please generate, in English: quest title, a 2-3 sentence quest description, 3 objective steps, opening dialogue, closing dialogue. Tone: tense, quietly wrong rather than dramatic — the absence of violence is the disturbing part. No mention of Dark Age of Camelot or DAOC-specific proper nouns."
