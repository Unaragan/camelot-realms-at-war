## Layout concept

- **3 Reichs spawn points** at the edges of the cards (Albion, Midgard, Hibernia), each with
  small portal festival/castle as a retreat/respawn point.
- **Mountain ranges** along the three sectoral boundaries between the rich — natural
  Separation, preventing direct lines of sight between the spawns, forcing the way across the
  Centre.
- **Rivers**, one each per realms-Sector, lead towards Zentralburg – visual
  Orientation, potentially movement hindrance/deceleration.
- **Intermediate destinations per approach route** (taken from the Moodboard): Siege camp,
  Watchtower, small outposts — capturable points, e.g. benefits when storming
  the central castle (rally point, resources, line of sight).
  Decor designations from the moodboard through clearly game-mechanically anchored stations.
- **Central Relic Castle ("Central Relic Castle")**:
  - Water trenches (outermost ring)
  - External wall with towers
  - **Zwingers** (Kill-Zone-Ring between outer and inner wall) - attackers who
    break through the outer wall, stand in the fire of the inner wall before they
    Reaching the real gate
  - Inner Wall, Bergfried/Donjon in the center as relictort
  - Bridges/gates as the only access via the trench, one access per kingdom (in the moodboard)
    only 2 visible bridges for 3 empires — if implemented, clarify whether 3 symmetrical
    Accesses or deliberately asymmetric/2 bridges plus footpath

## Frontier design: forced contact instead of separate zones

DAOC's classical frontier is really divided into its own border areas per realm —
Realm-vs-Realm-Contact usually occurs only at a few advance/boundary points, large parts
of their own side are PvE calm. for this project expressly **Otherwise desired**:
The three Reich sectors of this frontier zone are to be **None** Purely separated
Hinterland, but designed so that players from different empires can
also outside the central castle inevitably encounter — e.g. common intermediate destinations
(Siege warehouse/layout outpost), resource points, or
passage passes that are not clearly assigned to an empire, instead of strictly
So the mountain ranges don't separate three completely isolated
Paths to the castle, but rather create needle eye/divided transitions where contact
Probably rather than avoidable.

**Consequence for the layout:** When implementing, check whether, for example, river crossings,
Pass roads through the mountain ranges or individual intermediate destinations consciously
**rich neutral/shared** lie (not clearly in the sector of a single empire),
Two or three kingdoms must reach the same point.

## Open World Frontier vs. Battleground

[README.md](../../README.md) Phase 2 describes the **First Battleground** Conscious
different — small, symmetrical, a central target (flag/node), grayboxing reaches, model
DAOC's small RvR zones (Thidranki, Braemar), two factions at launch, third
(Hibernia) comes later.[CLASSES.md](../../CLASSES.md) Phase 2 section. This is
small BG is and remains the current task – this document does not change that.

This vision here belongs to another, later category: the **Normally open
World of the Three Kingdoms**, the README.md under "Afterwards: Deciding if/how direction"
"Expand the larger world" is mentioned. Open world, mountains, rivers, full
Castle Siege Mechanics (Zwinger, etc.), all three realms at the same time — this is
conceptually closer to DAOC’s large frontier zones than to Thidranki/Braemar, and thus
a stand-alone later feature, not a replacement for roadmap point 4.

**Recommendation:** current vertical slice scope (small symmetrical 2-fraction BG)
First finish and prove the core of the battle.
Maintain the open-world frontier phase.
