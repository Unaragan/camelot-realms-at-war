── SONG TITLE ────────────────────────────────────────────────

Come Home, Warrior (Raven Astrid)

── LYRICS ────────────────────────────────────────────────────

[Intro — solo war drum, 4 slow beats, low bagpipe drone fades in]

(whispered, like a raven speaking)
Huginn saw you put your sword down.
Muninn never forgot your name.

[Verse 1 — RAP — low bagpipe drone, sparse chord, Albion]

You left the battlefield when the season turned cold
told yourself the war was done, you were getting too old
but Odin's ravens circle still above the white shore
Huginn brings the thought — and the thought says: one more

Albion's banners hang where you left them in stone
your name carved in the gate of a kingdom alone
the knights still hold the line in the frost and the rain
and every single one of them is calling your name

You thought you walked away — nah, you just learned to hide
a warrior doesn't retire, they just step to the side
and the side gets smaller when the realm starts to bleed
Freyja chose you once — and she remembers your creed

[Pre-Chorus — jaw harp enters, drum pulse doubles]

(spoken, intense)
The ravens don't circle the dead.
They circle the ones who aren't done yet.

[Chorus — sung, haunting, melodic — all instruments together but sparse]

Come home, warrior
the ravens are calling your name
Albion, Hibernia — they remember your flame
Midgard beats the war drum
Freyja watches the gate

Come home, warrior
Valhalla can wait —
your realm needs you now
come home

[Verse 2 — RAP — Irish tin whistle single line, Hibernia]

Hibernia whispers through the mist and the green
the druids still walk where the old ones have been
you felt the forest breathe when you fought on that ground
the emerald realm is bleeding — and you're nowhere to be found

Listen — I'm not asking you to bleed for a king
I'm asking you to remember what that battle meant
the rush of holding the line when the odds were insane
the tribe you rode with, the fire, the pain

You traded that for what — for quiet, for ease?
while your old comrades fight alone in the cold and the freeze
Hibernia calls you through the tin whistle's cry
a single haunting note — it's asking you why

[Pre-Chorus — all rhythm builds, no melody, only pulse]

(spoken, raw)
You still know the path.
You still hear the horn.
That part of you never died —
it's just waiting to be reborn.

[Chorus — sung, haunting, melodic]

Come home, warrior
the ravens are calling your name
Albion, Hibernia — they remember your flame
Midgard beats the war drum
Freyja watches the gate

Come home, warrior
Valhalla can wait —
your realm needs you now
come home

[Bridge — drums alone — then RAP a cappella, no music]

(solo war drum — 8 beats — then silence)

(rapping, voice only, close and direct)

You were a knight of Albion —
honor in your bones, steel in your hand
you breathed the ancient forest — Hibernia's own
you swore on Midgard's iron to protect your land

Odin gave you ravens to remember who you are
Freyja gave you fire so you'd never fall too far
and every scar you carry is a story not yet done
the legend isn't over — it has barely just begun

(one jaw harp note — one tin whistle note — one drum hit)

(sung, barely above a whisper)
The gates are open.
Your name is still there.
Come home.

[Final Chorus — drums lead, voice leads, NO orchestral swell — power through restraint]

Come home, warrior
the ravens are calling your name
Albion, Hibernia — they remember your flame
Midgard beats the war drum
Freyja watches the gate

Come home, warrior
the war isn't over
come home, warrior
take back what you swore for

Come home —
Valhalla is watching —
come home.

[Outro — one drum hit. Silence. Three tin whistle notes only.]

(Raven Astrid, voice alone)

The realm remembers every name.
Odin's ravens never forget.

...come home, warrior.
