── SONG TITLE ────────────────────────────────────────────────

Call to Fight (Albion)

── LYRICS ────────────────────────────────────────────────────

[Intro — solo war horn, distant bell, herald voice alone]

(spoken, low and clear)
The banners have waited long enough.

[Verse 1 — baritone lead, harp underneath]

By the white cliffs where the old king sleeps,
the crimson standard still the border keeps,
but the watch grows thin and the gate stands wide,
and the realm is calling every sword that hid

We laid our shields down when the peace held true,
told the young ones the old wars were through,
but Midgard's drums beat closer every night,
and Hibernia's wolves are circling the light

[Pre-Chorus — choir joins, low, building]

Stand up, knight, the line remembers you,
stone doesn't forget what crimson blood is due,
the crown was never worn by one alone —
it's carried home on every shoulder, every throne

[Chorus — full choir, brass, drums]

CALL TO FIGHT, call to fight,
crimson banners rise into the light,
we are Albion, unbroken and true,
the realm still stands because of you

CALL TO FIGHT, call to fight,
every knight who left comes home tonight,
the gate is open, the horn has blown,
Albion was never yours alone

[Verse 2 — baritone, strings entering]

I saw a boy become a man in the mud,
saw a banner dyed twice over in blood,
and still they stood when the ranks grew thin —
that's not a kingdom, that's kin against kin for kin

So if you laid your armor by the door,
if you told yourself you won't fight anymore,
hear the horn, it isn't asking twice,
Albion remembers, and Albion pays the price

[Bridge — strings alone, quiet, building]

(sung, softer)
Not for the crown. Not for the gold.
For the ones still standing when the story's told.

[Final Chorus — full choir + orchestra, key change to D major]

CALL TO FIGHT, call to fight,
crimson banners rise into the light,
we are Albion, unbroken and true,
the realm still stands because of you

CALL TO FIGHT, call to fight,
every knight who left comes home tonight,
the gate is open, the horn has blown,
Albion was never yours alone

[Outro — horn fades, single bell, silence]

(spoken, final)
Come home, knight. The line is waiting.
