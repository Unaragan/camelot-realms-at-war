── SONG TITLE ────────────────────────────────────────────────

Call to Fight (Hibernia)

── LYRICS ────────────────────────────────────────────────────

[Intro — wind, distant birdsong, solo low whistle]

(whispered, like the forest itself speaking)
The old oak remembers every name carved beneath it.

[Verse 1 — female lead, fiddle entering soft]

We hung the green banners and let the woods grow still,
told the standing stones the war was against our will,
but the roots are restless under Hibernia's ground,
and the forest is humming a low and ancient sound

The groves stand empty where the clans used to sing,
the druids still whisper but the echo's thinning,
the stones remember every oath that was sworn,
and the wolves at the border say the fight isn't gone

[Pre-Chorus — bodhrán enters, harmonies join]

(sung, rising)
Rise, child of the grove, the forest calls you home,
the green never asked you to fight alone

[Chorus — full harmonies, fiddle soaring, bodhrán driving]

CALL TO FIGHT, call to fight,
the green rises with the fading light,
we are Hibernia, wild and free,
the forest remembers, and so do we

CALL TO FIGHT, call to fight,
every wanderer comes home tonight,
the stones are singing, the roots run deep,
Hibernia's oath is a vow we keep

[Verse 2 — fuller, fiddle weaving faster]

I saw an old druid lay her staff in the moss,
told the young ones the old ways bear too great a cost,
but she picked it back up when the border-fires burned —
that's not a warrior's choice, that's a debt long-earned

So if you laid your bow in the hollow oak tree,
if you told the wild wood it's no longer for thee,
hear the roots, sister — they're not asking twice,
Hibernia remembers, and Hibernia pays the price

[Bridge — solo fiddle, voice alone, quiet]

(sung, soft)
Not for the crown, not for the gold in the glen,
for the grove still singing when we're called again

[Final Chorus — full clan voice, fiddle and pipes together]

CALL TO FIGHT, call to fight,
the green rises with the fading light,
we are Hibernia, wild and free,
the forest remembers, and so do we

CALL TO FIGHT, call to fight,
every wanderer comes home tonight,
the stones are singing, the roots run deep,
Hibernia's oath is a vow we keep

[Outro — fiddle fades to solo whistle, wind returns]

(whispered, final)
Come home, wanderer. The grove is waiting.
