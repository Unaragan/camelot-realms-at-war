── SONG TITLE ────────────────────────────────────────────────

Call to Fight (Midgard)

── LYRICS ────────────────────────────────────────────────────

[Intro — throat singing drone, tagelharpa, single war drum hit]

(chanted, low)
Hear the ice crack. Hear the shield wall call.

[Verse 1 — lead growl/chant, drums building]

We buried the axes when the winter was long,
told the fire we were done, told ourselves we were wrong,
but the frost-wolves are howling past the Midgard line,
and the sons of the shield wall don't stay behind

Blue banners torn on the wind off the sea,
every hall stands empty where a warrior should be,
the mead is still poured but the benches sit bare,
and the war-chief is calling — is anybody there?

[Pre-Chorus — gang vocals join, drums doubling]

(shouted, together)
UP! Up from the fire, up from the snow!
The shield wall remembers where the strong ones go!

[Chorus — full gang vocals, war drums, horn blasts]

CALL TO FIGHT! CALL TO FIGHT!
Midgard rises in the frozen light!
Shield to shield, we hold the line —
the wall remembers, the wall is mine!

CALL TO FIGHT! CALL TO FIGHT!
Every warrior comes home tonight!
The horn has sounded, the ice has cracked —
Midgard never breaks, Midgard strikes back!

[Verse 2 — lower, grittier]

I stood at the shield wall the day it near broke,
watched an old man swing till his axe-arm just woke
something buried in him, something the fire couldn't kill —
that's not a warrior's choice, that's a warrior's will

So if you hung your axe on the wall by the door,
if you told the young ones you don't fight no more,
hear the ice, brother — it's not asking twice,
Midgard remembers, and Midgard pays the price

[Bridge — throat singing solo, drums drop out]

(low, guttural)
Not for the gold in the frost-locked ground.
For the shield wall standing when the horn's last sound.

[Final Chorus — full force, all voices, drums at full power]

CALL TO FIGHT! CALL TO FIGHT!
Midgard rises in the frozen light!
Shield to shield, we hold the line —
the wall remembers, the wall is mine!

CALL TO FIGHT! CALL TO FIGHT!
Every warrior comes home tonight!
The horn has sounded, the ice has cracked —
Midgard never breaks, Midgard strikes back!

[Outro — single war drum hits, fading, throat singing drone returns]

(chanted, final)
Come home, warrior. The wall is waiting.
